How to understand when the market rises or falls

This page is separate from individual cryptocurrency pages. It explains broad market behavior and teaches how to read market conditions without focusing on one specific coin.

01

When does the market go up?

A broad market rise means total market value is increasing. It can happen when demand and liquidity improve, sentiment changes, or important economic, regulatory or market news changes expectations.

  • Market capitalization rises
  • Trading activity may increase
  • More assets can show positive movement
  • A strong move can still reverse
02

When does the market go down?

A broad decline means total market value is decreasing. Selling pressure, lower liquidity, risk-off sentiment, regulation, macroeconomic changes or negative news can contribute.

  • Market capitalization falls
  • More assets may show negative movement
  • Volume can rise during strong selling
  • Short-term uncertainty can increase

READ THE MARKET

Five things to check

Market cap

Shows total market value. Compare it across time to understand broad direction.

Volume

Shows trading activity and helps put a price move into context.

Market breadth

Shows whether a move is broad or concentrated in a smaller part of the market.

Volatility

Shows how strongly prices move and helps describe uncertainty.

History

Compare 24H, 7D and 30D periods so one short move is not treated as the whole trend.

EDUCATIONAL RULE

One indicator is never the whole story.

Gugee's Analysis page is descriptive and educational. It does not tell you to buy or sell. Historical movement does not guarantee what happens next.
