MARKET GUIDE

What makes the market go up or down?

Market movement is not tied to one coin. Broad moves can come from changes in demand, trading volume, liquidity, macroeconomic conditions, regulation, major news and shifts in investor sentiment.

01When the market risesMarket value and demand are increasing across a wider part of the market.
02When the market fallsRisk appetite, liquidity or demand can decrease and selling pressure can spread.
03How to read itCompare market cap, volume, breadth and historical movement instead of one price alone.
